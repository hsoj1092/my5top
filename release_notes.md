# v1.0.0 — Initial Release: NJ Transit Anime Commute Assistant 🚆✨

### Highlights
- **1-Tap Work & Home Commute Board**: Pre-configured for **NY Penn Station (`NY`) — Work** and **Metropark (`MP`) — Home** with instant route swapping, Express vs. Local badges (`新快速 • NEC EXP` / `普通 • NEC LOCAL`), and full NJ Transit station support.
- **Live Track Assignments & Track Watcher**: View DepartureVision track numbers (`TRACK 9`, `TRACK 7`) or tap **Watch Track** on `TRACK TBA` trains to receive an immediate high-priority alert the second your track is announced.
- **"I'm on this train!" Active Ride Mode**:
  - Animated Chibi Train gliding along the Northeast Corridor stop timeline.
  - **Hybrid GPS + Live Schedule Stop Countdown**: Combines on-device GPS station geofencing with stop departure tracking to count down stops remaining and live ETA.
  - **Interactive Ride Simulator**: Test stop progression (`+1 Stop` or `Auto-Play Ride`) and track announcements (`Sim Track`) anytime.
- **Customizable Stop Notifications**:
  - Choose **"Start notifying me X stops away"** ($1$–$6$ stops before your destination).
  - Toggle **"Notify on every single stop"** and **"Final Wake-Up Arrival Alarm"** with custom vibration patterns.
- **Switchable Anime Themes**:
  - Toggle anytime between **Starry Rain** (`Tomori-navi` • Deep slate-blue & starry cyan with rain/star particles) and **Sakura Express Pastel** (`Hana-chan` • Warm sakura pink & sky blue with falling petals), plus Dark, Light, and Pure Black AMOLED modes.
- **Portable JSON Backups & GitHub APK Self-Updater**:
  - Export and restore `.my5top.json` backups directly to your Google Drive folder or local device storage via the native Android folder/file picker.
  - Built-in OTA APK updater from GitHub Releases.
